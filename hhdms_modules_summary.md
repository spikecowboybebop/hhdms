# Home Health Delivery Management System (HHDMS) - Module Summary Catalog

This document provides a concise catalog and high-level summary of all 13 core functional modules within the Home Health Delivery Management System (HHDMS).

---

## 3.1 Call Center Module
Handles inbound and outbound patient communications, real-time caller identification, patient registration, patient profile lookups, appointment booking/scheduling, and immediate emergency escalation routing.

## 3.2 MBBS Doctor Module - First Call (MB)
Acts as the primary clinical gateway. The MBBS doctor performs initial patient physical and clinical assessments, records vital signs, log preliminary diagnoses with ICD-10 support, orders diagnostic tests, manages lab integrations, and coordinates specialist care or referrals.

## 3.3 Specialist Doctor Module (SP)
Supports 11 specialist medical categories (such as Cardiology, Pulmonology, Neurology, Nephrology, etc.). Specialists review existing patient case files, vitals, and lab results to provide remote or in-person specialized medical consultations and advanced diagnostic orders.

## 3.4 Nurse Module (NS)
Manages the deployment and clinical data entry for specialized nursing services. This is split into Adult Nurses (providing clinical home care for patients 18 and older) and Pediatric Nurses (specializing in infants, neonates, and children under 18).

## 3.5 Nutritionist Module (NU)
Facilitates customized dietary assessments, tracks historical patient biometric data, generates personalized nutritional/dietary plans, and schedules calorie and meal tracking tailored to specific medical conditions.

## 3.6 Caregiver Module (CG)
Coordinates non-clinical daily living and personal support services tailored for Male, Female, and Child patients. Supports four distinct service durations: Day Care (8 hours), Night Care (8 hours overnight), 24-Hour Care, and Respite Care.

## 3.7 Diagnostic Services Module
Coordinates home-delivered mobile medical diagnostic services. This includes dispatching portable Ultrasound (USG) setups with a qualified Sonologist, as well as digital X-Ray imaging configurations managed by mobile technicians.

## 3.8 Live GPS Tracking & Dispatch Module (LT)
Provides real-time location visibility of all field staff to administrators, call center agents, and patients. It automates route optimization and sends a live web tracking link to the patient showing the provider's exact real-time Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).

## 3.9 Patient Management Module (PM)
Maintains comprehensive Electronic Health Records (EHR), multi-profile family linking, medical histories, allergies, master profiles, and past appointment logs.

## 3.10 Scheduling & Dispatch Module (SD)
Automates the smart assignment, booking schedules, and automated dispatch routing of healthcare providers based on specialization tags, geographic proximity, and shift availability calendar rules.

## 3.11 Billing & Payment Module (BP)
Manages system-wide billing workflows, multi-channel payment collections (MFS, credit cards, or cash), automated digital invoice generation, and individual provider commission split calculations.

## 3.12 Reporting & Analytics Module (RA)
Generates administrative dashboards, financial summaries, operational capacity audits, and clinical trend metrics to track overall service quality and business growth metrics.

## 3.13 Notification Engine (NF)
Triggers automated real-time SMS, WhatsApp messages, and mobile app push notifications for booking confirmations, shift updates, critical lab alerts, and administrative broadcasts.
